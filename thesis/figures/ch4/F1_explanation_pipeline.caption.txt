F1 — Explanation pipeline. Each alert day's composite (five agent scores) is decomposed exactly by Shapley values over the agents (efficiency residual ≤ 1.1e-16), each agent's score is broken into its own terms, region- and date-filtered precedents are retrieved, and a deterministic narrative whose claims are checked against the record feeds the transparent action rule. The SHAP surrogate is retained only as a comparator.
