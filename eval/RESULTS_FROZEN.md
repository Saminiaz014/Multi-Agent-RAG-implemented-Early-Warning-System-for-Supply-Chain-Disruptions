## Lead time

Post hoc, from `eval/lead_time.csv` (`eval/ch4/detection_figures.py`): the lead is the onset of a labelled run minus the first alert within 30 days either side of it, at each method's own operating point (alert when the score is at or above its training-window 90th percentile). Positive means the alert came before the onset; `*` means an alert was already active when the window opened.

**Every method, the random control included, leads every onset.** The label is a lagged 30-day mean (its onset follows the physical drop), and a method that alerts on many days has an alert before almost any onset. If alerts fell independently across days, a method alerting on 10% of days, as the random control does, would have an alert in the 30 days before an onset with probability 96%; real alerts are autocorrelated, so that is an upper reference. The highest alert rate in this table is 79%. Lead time here is not evidence of early warning.
